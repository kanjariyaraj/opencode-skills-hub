# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want this project to be a useful, respectful place for developers, designers, researchers, students and creators of all backgrounds. We pledge to make participation harassment-free for everyone, regardless of experience level, identity, background or ability.

## Expected behaviour

- Be direct and kind. Critique ideas and evidence, not people.
- Assume good faith, but ask for evidence when a claim matters.
- Respect differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Give credit to upstream authors and contributors.
- Protect privacy. Do not publish other people's private information.
- Follow the authorization rules for security work.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, intimidation, discrimination or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Trolling, insulting comments or deliberate disruption.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Submitting malicious install scripts, hidden payloads or credential stealers.
- Encouraging unauthorized access, scanning or exploitation of third-party systems.
- Dismissing security concerns without review.

## Scope

This code applies in repository spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and in public spaces when representing the project.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer listed in [AUTHORS.md](AUTHORS.md). Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially as possible. Do not open a public issue for a personal conduct report.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may:

1. Ask for a behaviour to stop.
2. Remove or edit comments, commits or contributions.
3. Temporarily or permanently restrict participation.

Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately and focused on repairing harm.

## Attribution

This document is informed by the Contributor Covenant and common open-source community standards. It is intentionally short and practical.
